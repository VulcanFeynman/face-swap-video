# Face Swap Video

**Put yourself in trending dance & runway videos**

🌐 Website: [Face Swap Video](https://faceswap.video/)

## About

Face Swap Video is a browser-based trending video studio that puts your photos into ready-made performances. Browse templates across dance, runway and music looks, watch the real source clip that guides motion and timing, then upload one clear image for each role you want to replace. Vehicle-style templates can take an object reference instead of a person.

## Features

- Photo-based face swap video templates
- Object, character and outfit replacement
- Dance, runway and music video templates
- 480p trending video output with matching soundtrack
- Source-guided duration up to about 30 seconds
- Compare source vs remix, then download MP4

## Get started

Visit [https://faceswap.video/](https://faceswap.video/) to try Face Swap Video in your browser — no install required.
